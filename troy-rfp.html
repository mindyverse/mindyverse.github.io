RFP Compliance & Response Readiness Case Study

An independent analysis of Troy University RFP #26-004 for CRM Software, focused on organising requirements, submission obligations, risks, clarifications and response-planning work into a clear and traceable framework.

Project purpose

What I wanted to understand

I wanted to understand how a business analyst or proposal analyst would review a complex RFP, organise the requirements, identify submission obligations, track risks and clarifications, and prepare the information needed before a real proposal is written.

Tools & skills

What I used

Microsoft ExcelMicrosoft WordRequirements AnalysisRFP AnalysisRisk IdentificationResponse Planning
My process

From a long RFP to a structured response-readiness framework.

01 • Reviewed the sources

I worked through the base RFP and both official addenda, checking the scope, proposal instructions, evaluation criteria and later clarifications.

02 • Organised the requirements

I converted the main requirements into a compliance matrix and recorded their source pages, obligation level, likely owner and information needed.

03 • Separated the response work

I created separate views for submission items, risks and clarifications, response planning and the overall opportunity summary.

Key deliverables

What I created

  • Compliance Matrix covering 45 CRM requirements.
  • Source Register for the base RFP and official addenda.
  • Submission Checklist for proposal documents and delivery requirements.
  • Risk & Clarification Log for open, changed and uncertain items.
  • Response Plan showing how a real bid could be organised.
  • Opportunity Brief and Executive Summary.
What I learned

The main lesson was traceability.

The project helped me understand how a long procurement document can be broken into smaller requirements and tracked back to the source. I also learned to separate confirmed requirements from assumptions. Where vendor-specific information was not available, I recorded it as vendor input needed instead of inventing an answer.

This is an independent learning and portfolio case study based on a publicly available RFP. It was not part of an actual Troy University bid submission.

Project note

How I handled missing information

I did not create fictional vendor capabilities, pricing, certifications or compliance claims. Any area that would normally need information from a real vendor was marked as vendor input needed or clarification required.
